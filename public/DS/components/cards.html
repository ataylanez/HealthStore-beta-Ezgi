Cards

The NHS card is the only container pattern in the product: flat, bordered, square, no shadow. Navigation uses the clickable card (via ClickableChevronCard), lists of cards use nhsuk-card-group on the NHS grid, and content cards (catalogue results, funding directory, case studies) are plain nhsuk-cards.

nhsuk-card, nhsuk-card--clickable, nhsuk-card--secondary, nhsuk-card__content--primary, nhsuk-card-group

Card group (grid of cards)

Lists of cards are a ul.nhsuk-grid-row.nhsuk-card-group whose items are li.nhsuk-grid-column-one-third.nhsuk-card-group__item (or one-half). Items stretch to equal height. Used on the home page, catalogue hub, resource library, funding index and supplier portal.

  </li>
  …
</ul>

Content card (catalogue result)

A plain nhsuk-card stacked full-width in the two-thirds results column: product name as nhsuk-card__heading nhsuk-heading-m link, supplier in nhsuk-body-s, description, tags, then a primary button and the compare toggle. Not clickable as a whole — it contains two actions.

Section headers

Section headings are plain NHS headings. A page section is an <h2 class="nhsuk-heading-l">; sub-sections are nhsuk-heading-m / nhsuk-heading-s. The gradient-rule SectionHeader component is retired from the PDP and is only still rendered by ProductVideosSection.

Canonical
nhsuk-heading-l (h2) · nhsuk-heading-m (h3) · nhsuk-heading-s (h4)
PDP helper
PdpSection renders <h2 class="nhsuk-heading-l hs-pdp-heading">; .hs-pdp-heading only adds the anchor scroll offset
Legacy
SectionHeader in components/Badges.tsx — candidate for removal

Section with caption

When a section needs context above its title use a caption inside the heading, matching the page-title pattern.

Projected impactWhat Luscii could mean for NHS Example ICB

<h2 class="nhsuk-heading-l">
  <span class="nhsuk-caption-l">Projected impact</span>
  What Luscii could mean for NHS Example ICB
</h2>

Rules

  • One nhsuk-heading-xl per page; sections start at nhsuk-heading-l and nest downwards without skipping levels.
  • No decorative rules, gradients or eyebrow labels above headings.
  • A description under a heading is ordinary body text, not small grey text.
  • Use nhsuk-u-margin-top-* between sections rather than bespoke section classes.

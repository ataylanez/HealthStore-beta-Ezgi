Alerts & notifications

Inline messages use the two NHS components for this job: inset text for information and warning callout for things the reader must not miss. Transient feedback uses a single global toast. The bespoke tinted alert boxes and page banners are retired.

Inset text

For information that is useful but not critical: supplier-reported caveats, empty states (“No apps match your filters”), notes under a summary. Thick NHS-blue left keyline, no background.

Information:

This entry is supplier-reported. Verify details with the supplier before procurement.

Warning callout

For things that could cause harm or block a decision: a clinical safety alert, DTAC not confirmed, or an “Action needed” review note in the supplier portal. Yellow label tab, pale-yellow panel.

Important

DTAC is required for this product but has not been confirmed by the supplier.

<div class="nhsuk-warning-callout" role="alert">
  <h3 class="nhsuk-warning-callout__label">
    <span role="text"><span class="nhsuk-u-visually-hidden">Important: </span>Important</span>
  </h3>
  <p>DTAC is required for this product but has not been confirmed by the supplier.</p>
</div>

Details (progressive disclosure)

Where a note is long or only sometimes needed (chart assumptions, verification steps) use nhsuk-details rather than a collapsible card.

Toasts

Transient confirmations and failures (“Added to compare”, “The comparison tool is full”, “Copied link”). One ToastProvider is mounted in AppShell; any client component calls useToast(). Toasts are bespoke (NHS has no toast) and are kept to a single bottom-centre stack, auto-dismissing, with a polite live region.

Accessibility

  • Danger-level callouts use role="alert"; informational ones use role="status".
  • Inset text and callouts carry a visually hidden “Information:” / “Important:” prefix so the meaning is not conveyed by colour alone.
  • Toasts announce via a polite live region and never trap focus.

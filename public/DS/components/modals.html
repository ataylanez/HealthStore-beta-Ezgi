Two consumers remain: the AI advisor drawer and the Clear prototype data confirmation. Express interest is no longer a modal (it is a multi-step journey) and the share panel was removed.

Accessibility checklist

  • role="dialog" + aria-modal="true" + aria-labelledby on the title.
  • Focus moves into the dialog on open and is trapped inside while open.
  • Escape closes; focus returns to the triggering control.
  • Background content is inert / not tabbable.

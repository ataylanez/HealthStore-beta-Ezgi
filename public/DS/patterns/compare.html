Comparison tool

Side-by-side evaluation of up to four digital therapeutics from the same condition area. Users build a basket from the catalogue or product pages with Add to compare, then read a series of NHS responsive tables, one per decision topic.

What changed. The “decision workspace” (task lens, differences-only toggle, collapsible attribute groups, horizontal scroll) was replaced by plain narrative tables in Round 9, and in Round 10 the page moved into the NHS 960px container with the intro in a two-thirds column. See Archived.

Anatomy

  1. Breadcrumb — Home / Comparison tool.
  2. Title and intro — nhsuk-heading-xl and an nhsuk-body-l paragraph in a two-thirds column explaining the four-product, same-condition rule.
  3. Toolbar — a nhsuk-heading-xs “Compared condition: COPD” line and a Clear all link-button (44px target).
  4. Narrative tables (CompareNarrativeTables) — six nhsuk-table-responsive tables with an nhsuk-table__caption--m: What it is · Evidence and expected impact · Assurance · What it takes to work locally · NHS experience · Cost and commercial route. Products are columns (header shows name, supplier and a Remove button); dimensions are row headers.
  5. Empty state — a sentence and a “Go to product catalogue” link; no card or illustration.

Table treatment

Below the tablet breakpoint the NHS responsive table stacks each cell with its column heading (nhsuk-table-responsive__heading) so nothing scrolls sideways. Cells that have no value say “Check with supplier” in secondary text rather than showing a dash. Links to the relevant product page sit in the cell where more detail exists.

Add to compare

The entry point is the compare toggle (CompareToggleButton): an NHS button with aria-pressed that reads Add to compare / Added. The header shows a count badge next to “Comparison tool” while the basket is non-empty. Adding a product from a different condition area, or a fifth product, shows a toast explaining why it was blocked.

Loading state

CompareSkeleton holds the title, intro and first table shape while the basket hydrates.

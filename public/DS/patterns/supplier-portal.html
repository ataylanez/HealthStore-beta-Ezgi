Supplier portal

The signed-in area for suppliers (/supplier-portal). It is built almost entirely from NHS components: a card-group dashboard, task lists, one-question-per-page forms, summary-list check-your-answers pages and confirmation panels. This page replaces the retired “Dashboard” pattern (commissioner dashboards were removed from the product).

Anatomy

  1. Sign in (/supplier-portal/sign-in) — SupplierSignInForm: two-thirds column, nhsuk-heading-xl, NHS inputs, primary button. Gate is prototype-only (PortalGuard, localStorage).
  2. Dashboard (/supplier-portal) — supplier name as nhsuk-caption-l above nhsuk-heading-xl “Dashboard”, an nhsuk-body-l intro, an attention list (DashboardAttentionList, two-thirds) of items needing action, then a nhsuk-card-group of five ClickableChevronCards: Company information, Product management, Published pages, Contact, Account management.
  3. Section hubs (/products, /published) — same card-group recipe; tables (ManagedProductsTable, OnboardingFormsTable, PublishedPagesTable, StatusTable) list items with a PortalStatusTag.
  4. Onboarding a product (/products/onboard/[formId]) — an NHS nhsuk-task-list (SupplierOnboardingTaskList) of tasks; each task is a sequence of one-question pages (TaskQuestionPage) ending in a check-your-answers page and a confirmation panel.
  5. Editing a field (/products/manage/[productId]/[field]) — ProductFieldEditor: product name as nhsuk-caption-l, question as the page title, NHS form group with hint and error message, NhsFileUpload for documents, Save and continue.
  6. Check answers — nhsuk-summary-list with Change links (ProductCheckAnswers, CompanyCheckAnswers, PublishedPageCheckAnswers).
  7. Confirmation — green nhsuk-panel plus “What happens next” (ProductSubmissionConfirmation, CompanyConfirmation, PublishedPageConfirmation).
  8. Review states — ActionNeededCallout / FormActionNeededNotice (nhsuk-warning-callout) when the HealthStore team sends something back; ReviewStatusSummary for the current state.

Status tags

PortalStatusTag maps portal states to stock NHS tag colours — no new styles.

Loading state

PortalSkeletonPage holds the title and card-group shape while the portal state hydrates from localStorage.

Do

  • Ask one question per page; put the product or company name in a nhsuk-caption-l above the question.
  • Finish every task with check-your-answers and a confirmation panel.
  • Keep status language to the five states above.

Don't

  • Build widget or KPI dashboards — the attention list and card group are the whole dashboard.
  • Invent new tag colours for statuses.

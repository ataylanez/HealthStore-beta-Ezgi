Discovery & catalogue

How users find products: a catalogue hub of pathway cards (/catalogue) and a filterable product list (/catalogue/digital-therapeutics). Both use the NHS width container, grid and card components.

What changed in Round 10. The self-filling card grid and the GOV.UK-style filter bar with uppercase labels are gone. Filters sit in an NHS one-third column; results are full-width NHS cards stacked in the two-thirds column.

1. Catalogue hub

Title and lede in a two-thirds column, then NHS card groups: one clickable chevron card per live pathway, and nhsuk-card--secondary cards for pathways that are coming soon.

2. Product list — filters + results

Filter panel (.hs-filter-panel, one-third column): a nhsuk-heading-s “Filter” title, collapsible sections per facet, NHS nhsuk-radios--small for single-choice facets (condition) and nhsuk-checkboxes--small for multi-choice (supervision model), then an NHS Apply button and a “Clear filters” link. On mobile the panel collapses behind a Filter button. Active filters are shown above the results as removable pills.

Results (two-thirds column): a result count in nhsuk-body-m (bold number), then an nhsuk-list of nhsuk-cards. Each card: logo, product name as nhsuk-card__heading nhsuk-heading-m link, supplier in nhsuk-body-s, a one-sentence description, condition / supervision / maturity tags, then View product details (NHS primary button) and Add to compare (compare toggle, solid). The empty state is an nhsuk-inset-text.

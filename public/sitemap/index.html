What every page shares

A few things appear on every page of the Commissioning Store, so they are described once here.

The header and footer
  • The blue NHS header shows the service name “NHS Commissioning Store” — clicking it takes you back to the home page. Underneath sits a banner explaining this is a new service in development (“Alpha”).
  • The menu has five sections: About, Product catalogue, Comparison tool (with a count of products you have picked to compare), Funding index, and Resource library. On smaller screens the items that do not fit move into a “More” menu.
  • The footer links to the Cookies page and to the Supplier portal, and carries a short line explaining the site is a prototype.
  • A cookie banner appears until you choose to accept or reject analytics; your choice is remembered on your device. A “Back to top” button appears once you scroll down the page.
Technical details
Components
components/Nav.tsx (header and menu, with a ResizeObserver-driven overflow menu), components/AppShell.tsx (page shell, footer, providers), components/PhaseBanner.tsx, components/CookieBanner.tsx, components/BackToTop.tsx.
Client state
Compare basket in localStorage (healthstore-compare-basket, one condition at a time, up to four products); expressions of interest via /api/express-interest; cookie choice in localStorage; toast notifications.
Who can see the site

The whole site sits behind a single password. The first time you visit you are asked for it on the “private prototype” page; once entered, every page is open to you. The guidance articles in the Resource library are written for anyone, including clinicians.

Technical details
How it works
middleware.ts checks for the hs-gate cookie on every request and redirects to /gate?next=… until it is set. The password check happens at POST /api/gate, which sets the cookie for 30 days. API requests such as /api/express-interest return JSON rather than redirecting, and the static reference sites under /DS and /templates skip the check.
Behind the scenes (technical)

Two small services support the pages above.

ServiceWhat it does
POST /api/gateChecks the site password and remembers it on your device for 30 days.
GET/POST/DELETE /api/express-interestSaves, lists and clears expressions of interest submitted from product pages.

A few things appear on every page of the Supplier portal, so they are described once here.

The header and footer
  • The blue NHS header shows the service name “HealthStore Supplier portal” — clicking it takes you to the dashboard. On the right sit your name (which opens Account management) and a Sign out button.
  • The menu has three sections: Dashboard, Product management, and Published pages. On focused journeys — signing in, or answering questions one at a time — the menu row is hidden so you can concentrate.
  • The footer is the same as the Commissioning Store’s: links to the Cookies page and the Supplier portal, with a short line explaining the site is a prototype.
Technical details
Components
components/Nav.tsx (portal menu entries and the account area), components/supplier-portal/PortalGuard.tsx (sign-in gate for every portal page), components/AppShell.tsx (page shell and footer, with a portal styling class).
Who can see it

The portal sits behind the same site password as the rest of the prototype, and then asks you to sign in. This prototype accepts any email address and password; you stay signed in on your device until you sign out.

Technical details
How it works
PortalGuard redirects any signed-out visit to /supplier-portal/sign-in?next=… and sends you back afterwards. The signed-in state lives in the browser (localStorage key hs-supplier-portal:state); nothing renders until that state has been read, to avoid a flash of signed-in content.
Behind the scenes (technical)

There is no server or back office: everything you do in the portal is saved in your browser, and anything you submit is accepted straight away.

WhereWhat it holds
localStorage hs-supplier-portal:stateSign-in state, company information answers, product drafts and submissions, and the status of every store page.
localStorage hs-supplier-portal:onboarding:{formId}Your answers to a product onboarding form, one entry per form.

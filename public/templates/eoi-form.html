EOI (Form)

Transactional flow. One question per page, then check your answers, then confirmation. Each step is its own address and uses the two-thirds question-page layout.

Notes

Wagtail consideration note: Each step is its own route, but the flow itself stays in site code: validation, step guards, answer state and email verification are logic, not content. The question copy, hints and confirmation text are what the Back Office team edits — expose these as editable strings or snippets rather than modelling the steps as CMS pages. Wagtail’s form builder is a poor fit for a multi-step journey that verifies an email address.

Supplier onboarding and the supplier portal editors ask one question per page in the same layout. They are not part of this template: they are site functionality for suppliers, described on the Supplier portal pattern.

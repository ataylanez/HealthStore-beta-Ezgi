The public screens collapse into 8 templates. Of those, 5 are CMS content templates (Home, Content page, Hub, Directory, Product detail page): the pages the Back Office team would create and edit as part of their general tasks. Current thinking is the Product detail page will be a hybrid: a data-backed Wagtail page type whose product proposition and content narrative are CMS-edited alongside the structured product record. The remaining 3 are driven by product data or website functionality: the flows and tools stay in site code, with copy exposed as CMS-editable strings where the tags on each template page indicate.

Every template uses the same NHS frame: a 960px page width shared with the header and navigation, the breadcrumb above the main area, an extra-large page title, NHS lede text for the intro, and a two-thirds column for reading and forms. Side-by-side pages (the catalogue filters, a product’s contents list) put a one-third column beside it. What changed in the templates explains how this differs from the 5 August descriptions.

Notes

Wagtail consideration note: Best modelled as a singleton HomePage type with a StreamField of section blocks (hero, content-card row, numbered steps, pathway links, case-study band). Section order and copy stay editable without developer involvement; the pathway cards and case-study band reference other content rather than duplicating it. Card rows render as NHS card groups, not a free-form grid.

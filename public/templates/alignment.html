Templates

What changed in the templates

There are still eight templates. Every public page now uses the same NHS layout. A few pages were missing from the August map.

The template overview and each template page have been updated.

What stayed the same

  • Eight templates: Home, Content page, Hub, Directory, Product listing, Product detail, Expression of interest, Comparison tool.
  • Five are edited in the CMS: Home, Content page, Hub, Directory, Product detail. Three are built from product data or site code: Product listing, Expression of interest, Comparison tool.
  • The supplier portal is not a CMS template. It uses the same question pages as the expression of interest. See the Supplier portal pattern.

Layout used by every template

  • The page is 960 pixels wide, the same width as the header, the navigation and the “Alpha” banner.
  • The breadcrumb, or the back link on a form, sits above the main content.
  • The page title is the large NHS heading. The sentence under it is the larger NHS intro, in sentence case.
  • Body text, forms and the funding list sit in a two-thirds column. Pages with a side column use a one-third column next to it.

Each template

T1 · Home

The hero sits inside the 960px width. The headline is in the two-thirds column, with the picture beside it. The cards for what the service addresses, how it works, and the pathways are equal height, in three columns, and stack on a phone. Case studies are still to be confirmed.

T2 · Content page

About and Cookies are a title, an intro, then sections of text, in the two-thirds column.

Resource pages use the same template:

  • Section pages: Training and guidance, Preparing to recommend a DTx, Prior to patient appointment, During the appointment, After a patient appointment. Each is a title, an intro, and a list of links.
  • Articles are a title and text in the two-thirds column.

“Long-term management of a DTx” is a card on the resource library. It has no page yet.

T3 · Hub

The catalogue and the resource library have a title and intro in the two-thirds column, then equal-height cards. Catalogue pathways that are not live yet are quieter cards in the same group. The resource library has six cards.

T4 · Directory

Funding is a list of cards in the two-thirds column. Open, upcoming and periodic items share one list. Closed items sit under “Closed (confirm current status)”. Editors still update each record on its own.

T5 · Product listing

Filters sit in a one-third column: condition (pick one), supervision model (pick any), then Apply. Results are stacked cards in the two-thirds column, each with “View product details” and “Add to compare”. If nothing matches, the page says “No apps match your filters”. The filters are the side column.

T6 · Product detail

A product is a set of pages, not one long page:

  • An overview at /apps/[slug].
  • One page for each section at /apps/[slug]/[section]: the problem, how it helps, local impact, what it could be worth, funding levers, assurance, implementation, NHS experience, how to buy, and resources.

The left column is a contents list. The current page is bold. The others are links. The supplier name sits in the product title. “Add to compare” sits with the title. “Express interest” stays at the bottom. Section titles come from one list, so the contents list and the headings stay in step. The local-value model is site code, not CMS copy.

T7 · Expression of interest

Each step is its own page: a back link, the question as the title, form fields, and a two-thirds column. If an answer fails a check, the errors are listed at the top and the field is marked. Check your answers is a summary list. Confirmation is the green panel. Question wording can be edited. The steps stay in site code. Supplier onboarding uses the same question pages and is not part of this set.

T8 · Comparison tool

The title and a short intro sit in the two-thirds column. Below that, up to six tables compare the chosen products, with products as columns. On a phone each cell is labelled with the product name. With nothing selected, the page shows a sentence and a link to the catalogue. There is no “compare by” switch and no “only differences” checkbox.
